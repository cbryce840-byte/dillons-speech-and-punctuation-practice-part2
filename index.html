Dylan's Quick Punctuation & Comma Reminder Guide:

❓ Question Mark (?) Used at the end of a sentence that asks something (e.g., "What is your name?").
. Period (.) Used at the end of a complete statement or fact (e.g., "The sun is shining.").
, Comma (,) Used when listing items or naming things in a series (e.g., "I bought apples, bananas, and oranges.").
! Exclamation (!) Used to show strong emotion, excitement, or surprise (e.g., "Look at that rocket soar!").
